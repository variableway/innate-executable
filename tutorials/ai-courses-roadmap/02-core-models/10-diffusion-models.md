# Lesson 10: Diffusion Models (Stable Diffusion Basics)

The physics-inspired approach to generation. Learning to reverse a noise process.

## Resources

### Articles
- [The Annotated Diffusion Model](https://huggingface.co/blog/annotated-diffusion) – **The single best resource for this topic.** It implements the "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (DDPM) paper from scratch with line-by-line commentary.

### YouTube
- [Diffusion Models | Paper Explanation | Math Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HoKDTa5jHvg) – Explains the physics-inspired math (thermodynamics) behind diffusion without getting lost in jargon.

### Notebooks
- [Classic DDPM from Scratch](https://github.com/labmlai/annotated_deep_learning_paper_implementations/tree/master/labml_nn/diffusion/ddpm) – Part of the "LabML Annotated Papers" series, which is excellent for code-first learners.

## Key Concepts
- Forward diffusion (adding noise)
- Reverse diffusion (denoising)
- Noise scheduling
- How Stable Diffusion works at the core
