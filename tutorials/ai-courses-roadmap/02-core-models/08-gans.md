# Lesson 8: GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)

The "Creative" side of AI. Two networks competing to generate realistic data.

## Resources

### GitHub
- [eriklindernoren/PyTorch-GAN](https://github.com/eriklindernoren/PyTorch-GAN) – Single-file implementations of almost every GAN variant (DCGAN, CycleGAN, Pix2Pix).

### YouTube
- [Build a GAN in 10 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O1sGR_XqsYs) – A rapid-fire simplified guide to the generator-discriminator game.

## Key Concepts
- Generator vs Discriminator adversarial game
- DCGAN, CycleGAN, Pix2Pix variants
- Mode collapse and training instability
- How GANs generate realistic data
